Isocast 0.0.5 for Apple-silicon Macs (macOS 14+). If you're on 0.0.4, Isocast › Check for Updates… installs it. Your camera, microphone and screen permissions carry over.

**Switch from OBS**
- **Import from OBS on first launch:** Isocast finds your OBS scene collections and brings them in with their profile's settings, then **Optimize** picks settings for Recording, Streaming or Both on your Mac.
- **obs-websocket v5:** turn it on with `isocast websocket enable` and tools built for OBS (Touch Portal, community Stream Deck plugins, obs-websocket scripts) drive Isocast.
- **Replay buffer:** save the last 30 seconds (up to 5 minutes) on a hotkey.
- **Virtual camera:** Isocast shows up as a camera in Zoom, FaceTime and other apps (macOS asks you to allow it once). Remove it any time from the camera popover.
- **Multistream:** stream to several destinations at once from one encode.

**Edit in Isocast**
- **The editor window** (Window › Editor, ⌘E): every ISO track of a take on one timeline. Trim, split, switch angles, and export frame-accurately without touching the originals.

**Recording**
- **Crash-safe takes, on by default:** if Isocast or your Mac crashes, you lose about half a second, and the files are repaired on the next launch.
- **Per-ISO audio:** choose each ISO file's audio (camera + mic, screen + desktop audio).
- **Per-app audio:** record one app's sound, or everything except some apps (macOS 14.2+).
- **ISO capacity warnings:** Isocast tells you when your Mac can't keep up with your ISO files, with a one-click fix, and picks sizes that fit.
- **Streaming after you start recording no longer costs ISO frames,** and the program and audio stay clean at your Mac's limit.
- **VP8 WebM stingers** (older OBS stingers) play without converting.

**Fixes**
- After an update, Isocast replaces an older engine still running (never mid-take).
- A microphone whose driver stops answering no longer blocks recording for minutes.
- Faster CLI commands; a hard-to-hit crash when listing silent media as ISO audio; macOS prompts now say "Isocast Engine".
