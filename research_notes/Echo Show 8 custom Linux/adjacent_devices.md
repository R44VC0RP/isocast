# Amazon adjacent device ecosystems (Fire tablets, Fire TV, Echo Show 15, other smart displays) and implications for running custom Linux on the Echo Show 8

Research date: 2026-10-11. Scope: what is known about unlocking/rooting Amazon's adjacent device ecosystems, which techniques worked, and transferability to the Echo Show 8. **Headline finding that reframes the whole report: the Echo Show 8 1st Gen (2019, codename `crown`, product AEOCW) is itself directly supported by an amonet BootROM exploit port and has shipping unofficial LineageOS 18.1 and /e/OS builds — it is not merely analogous to the Fire ecosystem, it *is* part of it.** The Echo Show 8 2nd Gen (MT8183) and 3rd Gen (Amlogic T7) are different stories.

## Fire tablets: amonet, mtk-su, Fire Toolbox, LineageOS — and why amonet worked

### Takeaway
The Fire tablet scene proved that every MediaTek MT8163-based Amazon device shares one fatal boot-chain property: a BootROM (BROM) flaw that no OTA update can patch, because the BootROM is mask ROM. The Echo Show 8 Gen 1 uses the exact same MT8163 platform as the original amonet targets, and the same exploit chain (ported by R0rt1z2) unlocks it. Fire tablets additionally show the two lower tiers of modification — ADB-based "system user" tweaks (Fire Toolbox) and temporary root (mtk-su) — both of which Amazon *can* and *did* patch via software updates.

### Cited Findings
- amonet is "an exploit chain for Fire HD 8 (2018) (8th gen / karnak / KFKAWI). It contains a MediaTek bootrom exploit and a LittleKernel bootloader exploit," released by XDA user xyzz; installation thread is XDA's "Fire HD 8 2018 downgrade/unlock/root" — [xyzz/amonet on GitHub](https://github.com/xyzz/amonet)
- The postmarketOS wiki explains the chain precisely: "Amonet is an lk exploit used to boot an unsigned kernel… This exploit is installed by a bootrom exploit," and notes devices must be forced into BootROM flash mode via hardware buttons/test points; on Fire 7 2019 (mustang, MT8163V/B) a later "hardware method" unlock/unbrick also exists — [postmarketOS wiki: Amazon Fire 7 2019 (amazon-mustang)](https://wiki.postmarketos.org/wiki/Amazon_Fire_7_2019_%28amazon-mustang%29)
- Timeline: the Fire HD 8 (8th gen, karnak) was rooted in early 2019, and "just a few weeks after the Fire HD 8 had been rooted" the Fire 7 (2019) got full unlock + TWRP + custom ROM (Feb 28, 2019) — [AFTVnews: Amazon Fire 7 tablet has been Rooted](https://www.aftvnews.com/tag/rom/)
- Exploiting karnak/suez-class devices requires opening the case and shorting a test point (CLK/GND or similar) while connecting USB to force BootROM mode, then running `step-1.sh` which "flashes a hacked bootloader and boots TWRP" — [blackhathq: How To Jailbreak Amazon Fire Tablet (Jul 22, 2026)](https://www.blackhathq.com/post/how-to-jailbreak-amazon-fire-tablet); same hardware-shorting procedure documented for the Fire TV Stick 2 in — [Liliputing (Mar 5, 2019)](https://liliputing.com/2nd-gen-amazon-fire-tv-stick-hack-unlock-the-bootloader-install-custom-recovery)
- Rootable vs not, by generation: Fire HD 8 (2018), Fire 7 (2017/2019), Fire HD 10 (2017, "suez", amonet-suez-v1.1.2) are fully rootable; "Fire HD 8/10 (2020+) 10th+ gen: Generally NOT rootable — no public exploit"; Fire OS 7+ (2019+) tablets "generally cannot be rooted. The bootloader is locked down tight. Amazon learned from the early models" — [blackhathq (Jul 22, 2026)](https://www.blackhathq.com/post/how-to-jailbreak-amazon-fire-tablet)
- mtk-su is "a superuser tool for Mediatek 64-bit Android devices, developed by diplomatic@XDA" giving **temporary** root from a running system; its underlying flaw was later cataloged as CVE-2020-0069 (Command Queue driver out-of-bounds write → LPE, CVSS 7.8), patched in the March 2020 Android Security Bulletin and later listed in CISA KEV (Nov 3, 2021) — [XDA: Amazing Temp Root for MediaTek ARMv8](https://xdaforums.com/t/amazing-temp-root-for-mediatek-armv8-2020-08-24.3922213); [XDA: mtk-su rootkit coverage](https://www.xda-developers.com/mediatek-su-rootkit-exploit); [CVE-2020-0069 tracker](https://notcve.org/cve/CVE-2020-0069); [Machiia/mitksu mirror](https://github.com/Machiia/mitksu)
- Fire Toolbox is a Windows app that modifies Fire OS purely via ADB with **no root and no exploit**: it removes lockscreen ads, disables Alexa/Kindle services, sideloads APKs (incl. Play Store), installs custom launchers/keyboards, and blocks OTA updates; privileges are temporary and reset on reboot; works on Fire OS 7/8 tablets and Fire TVs from 2019 onward — [blackhathq (Jul 22, 2026)](https://www.blackhathq.com/post/how-to-jailbreak-amazon-fire-tablet)
- A separate "system user" (UID 1000) ADB exploit by XDA member Pro-me3us (Sept 2025) supercharged Fire Toolbox/Launcher Manager capabilities (replace launcher/keyboard, disable Amazon apps); Amazon began patching it in Fire OS 8.1.5.3 for Fire TV in early Oct 2025, with a Fire OS 7 patch expected; vulnerable versions were Fire OS 7.3.3.1 / 8.3.3.6 and earlier — [Liliputing: Amazon patches the exploit that supercharged Fire TV and Fire Tablet hacking (Oct 6, 2025)](https://liliputing.com/amazon-patches-the-exploit-that-supercharged-fire-tv-and-fire-tablet-hacking)
- LineageOS for Fire tablets is **unofficial** (community builds by Rortiz2 and others, e.g. LineageOS 14.1 for suez, 16.1 for mustang), not in official LineageOS device lists; postmarketOS has an archived, partial port for mustang (screen/touch work, audio/USB-networking broken), with MT8163 **mainline** Linux work ongoing at github.com/mt8163-mainline/linux (serial console + simplefb working; Panfrost GPU driver for Mali-T720 merged in Linux 5.2 but downstream support incomplete) — [postmarketOS wiki: amazon-mustang](https://wiki.postmarketos.org/wiki/Amazon_Fire_7_2019_%28amazon-mustang%29); [mt8163-mainline/linux](https://github.com/mt8163-mainline/linux)
- **Direct transferability: the Echo Show 8 Gen 1 (crown, AEOCW) is listed as a supported device in R0rt1z2's amonet fork branch `mt8163-echo-show`** alongside Echo Show 5 1st Gen (checkers, AEOCH) and Echo Show 5 2nd Gen (cronos, AEOCN); the fork README describes it as "a MediaTek bootrom exploit and a Preloader exploit" for "some models of the Amazon Echo Show" — [R0rt1z2/amonet mt8163-echo-show README](https://github.com/R0rt1z2/amonet/blob/mt8163-echo-show/README.md)
- **Shipping LineageOS 18.1 for crown**: initial release v0.1 by bengris32 on Nov 6, 2025, maintained since by R0rt1z2 (v0.2 Nov 8, 2025 → v0.5); ROM notes: kernel v4.9.337 (android-4.9-q), deep sleep disabled, mute button acts as power button, battery always reports 100%, microphone quieter than stock, audio-HAL input-reset bug open (GitHub issue #4), Wi-Fi fixed in v0.4+; device trees published at github.com/amazon-oss (android_device_amazon_mt8163-common, android_device_amazon_crown, Apache-2.0) — [XDA: [ROM][UNOFFICIAL][11][crown] LineageOS 18.1 for the Amazon Echo Show 8 (2019)](https://xdaforums.com/t/rom-unofficial-11-crown-lineageos-18-1-for-the-amazon-echo-show-8-2019.4766709/page-2); [amazon-oss/releases](https://github.com/amazon-oss/releases/releases)
- An unofficial /e/OS-R 2.3 build for crown was released Nov 9, 2025 (ronnz98), and full camera enablement patches for Echo Show 5 1st/2nd gen + Echo Show 8 1st gen were published Feb 2026 (jxlarrea) — [/e/OS community](https://community.e.foundation/t/unofficial-build-amazon-echo-show-8-2019-crown-for-e-os-r-2-3/77270); [jxlarrea/lineageos-echo-show-camera](https://github.com/jxlarrea/lineageos-echo-show-camera)
- Echo Show 8 Gen 1 hardware reference (from the community install guide): MT8163 SoC, quad-core Cortex-A53, Mali-450 MP4 GPU, 2GB RAM, 8GB eMMC, micro-USB; UART on pin TP55 at 921600 baud; button mapping: Mute = power, Vol-Up = recovery, all three = fastboot; unlock requires the amonet `crown` build (amonet-crown-v1.0.0) — [winmutt/self-2026 LINEAGEOS_INSTALLATION_GUIDE.md](https://github.com/winmutt/self-2026/blob/master/LINEAGEOS_INSTALLATION_GUIDE.md)
- Confirmation it works on current firmware: a user reported "Amazon Echo Show 8 Gen1 Installed to latest OTA FireOS, Used amonet-crown-v1.0.0 lineage-18.1-20251108-UNOFFICIAL-crown.zip" (Nov 2025) — [XDA crown ROM thread, p.3](https://xdaforums.com/t/rom-unofficial-11-crown-lineageos-18-1-for-the-amazon-echo-show-8-2019.4766709/page-3)

### Inferences (transferability to Echo Show 8)
- **Gen 1 (crown): nothing to "transfer" — the full stack already exists.** The Echo Show 8 Gen 1 sits on the same MT8163 boot chain (BROM → preloader → Little Kernel) as the amonet Fire tablets, which is exactly why the port took weeks rather than years. A report on custom Linux for this device should cite amonet + LineageOS 18.1 as the existing baseline, not as speculation.
- Why amonet "worked" and keeps working: the entry-point flaw lives in mask ROM (unchangeable after silicon manufacture), so OTA updates can only harden later stages (preloader/LK), not the BROM. This explains why amonet-crown still worked on the latest Fire OS in Nov 2025, and implies Gen 1 devices receiving Amazon updates remain unlockable for the foreseeable hardware life.
- mtk-su and the Pro-me3us system-user exploit are the *patchable* tiers (kernel/Android userspace flaws) — Amazon demonstrably patches these within weeks-to-months (March 2020 bulletin; Fire OS 8.1.5.3 in Oct 2025). A "no-hardware-access" software-only route on Echo Show devices should be assumed short-lived.
- The realistic ceiling for "full custom Linux" on MT8163 today is a 4.9 Android kernel (LineageOS 18.1) plus WIP mainline work (mt8163-mainline: serial console, simplefb, early Panfrost); postmarketOS on the sibling mustang tablet is only "partial." A true mainline-Linux Echo Show 8 remains research-grade, consistent with the slower smart-display mainline ecosystem.
- Gen 2 (2021, MT8183) does **not** inherit the MT8163 amonet chain (different SoC, and no Echo Show 8 Gen 2 entry appears in the amonet fork or unlock threads); Gen 3 requires the hardware eMMC route (see Fire TV / Show 8 Gen 3 findings below).

### Gaps
- Exact release date of xyzz's original amonet for karnak (XDA thread t3894256 dates were not directly retrievable; AFTVnews's Feb 2019 Fire 7 article pins it to "a few weeks before" late Feb 2019).
- The precise technical nature of the amonet BROM flaw on MT8163 (the community references https://tinyhack.com/2021/01/31/dissecting-a-mediatek-bootrom-exploit/ for BROM-exploit internals, but that article covers the generic MTK SLA/DAA bypass rather than amonet specifically; no amonet-specific writeup was found).
- Whether any Fire HD 8/10 (2020+, 10th gen) exploit has appeared since the blackhathq summary (Jul 2026) — no evidence found, consistent with "no public exploit."

## Fire TV devices: unlock/custom ROM successes and boot chain comparison

### Takeaway
Fire TV devices proved the MediaTek boot-chain exploits scale across Amazon's whole product line — including full custom ROMs — but also that Amazon's countermeasures (requiring old Fire OS versions, new SoCs) grew more effective over time. The most striking recent result: a brand-new amonet port for the Fire TV Stick 4K 2nd Gen shipped **Aug 2026**, showing this attack class is still alive on current hardware.

### Cited Findings
- Fire TV Stick 2nd gen: bootloader unlock + custom recovery via the same amonet-class exploit (k4y0z), requires Linux, opening the case, and shorting CLK/GND test points (Mar 2019) — [Liliputing (Mar 5, 2019)](https://liliputing.com/2nd-gen-amazon-fire-tv-stick-hack-unlock-the-bootloader-install-custom-recovery)
- **kamakiri** (the BootROM exploit later generalized into the "MTK Auth Bypass (SLA/DAA) Utility" by k4y0z and Dinolek, published Feb 11, 2021) "was originally found by @xyz` [amonet's author] and released for the Amazon FireTV Stick 4K (mantis)"; the bypass utility supports a long list of SoCs including mt8127, **mt8163, mt8167, mt8173**, mt8695 and many phone chips — [XDA: MediaTek / MTK Auth Bypass (SLA/DAA) Utility](https://xdaforums.com/t/mod-dev-mediatek-mtk-auth-bypass-sla-daa-utility.4232377/page-10)
- Fire TV Stick 3 / Stick Lite (sheldon/sheldonp): unlocked via kamakiri (`bootrom-step.sh`, no disassembly needed) but **only on Fire OS 7.2.7.3 or older** — i.e., Amazon's OTA hardening of later boot stages already matters here; TWRP by k4y0z, LineageOS 18.1 (Android 11) by Rortiz2 released Mar 2, 2022, with known issues (SELinux permissive, Netflix crashes); a LineageOS 20 (Android 13) build exists (2026) — [AFTVnews (Mar 14, 2022)](https://www.aftvnews.com/fire-tv-stick-3-and-fire-tv-stick-lite-have-been-rooted-custom-twrp-revocery-and-custom-lineageos-android-11-rom-available/); [XDA Developers (Mar 13, 2022)](https://www.xda-developers.com/amazon-fire-tv-stick-3-lite-bootloader-unlock-android-11-lineageos-18-1); [XDA: LineageOS 18.1 sheldon thread](https://xdaforums.com/t/rom-unofficial-11-lineageos-18-1-for-fire-tv-stick-lite-3rd-gen-sheldon-p.4410317/page-13); [XDA: LineageOS 20 sheldon thread](https://xdaforums.com/t/rom-unofficial-13-lineageos-20-for-fire-tv-stick-lite-3rd-gen-sheldon-p.4792510)
- LineageOS 14.1 (Android 7.1.2) also exists unofficially for the Fire TV Stick 4K 1st gen (mantis) — [XDA: LOS 14.1 for mantis](https://xdaforums.com/t/rom-unofficial-7-1-2-lineageos-14-1-for-amazon-fire-tv-stick-4k-mantis.4705783)
- **Fire TV Stick 4K 2nd Gen family (karat, mantra, mantra_plus): amonet-karat-v1.3.0.zip by R0rt1z2, current release as of Aug 20, 2026** — "a bootloader unlock exploit… persistent and will replace the stock recovery with a custom one (TWRP), as well as provide you with an unlocked fastboot interface"; explicitly does NOT work on the 1st-gen series (kara, mantis); includes an OTG Y-cable boot menu; high brick risk warned — [XDA: [UNLOCK][ROOT][TWRP][UNBRICK] Fire TV Stick 4K 2nd Gen](https://xdaforums.com/t/unlock-root-twrp-unbrick-fire-tv-stick-4k-2nd-gen-series-karat-mantra.4798951); [ftvdb.com blog (Aug 20, 2026)](https://ftvdb.com/blog/2026-08-20-fire-tv-stick-4k-bootloader-unlock-twrp)
- Pre-rooted stock ROMs (Fire OS 5.2.7.3) by rbox for rooted Fire TV 1/2 and Stick 1/2 (Jul 2020) show the older Qualcomm-based Fire TVs also had a long rooting history — [AFTVnews (Jul 5, 2020)](https://www.aftvnews.com/tag/rom/)
- GhostLock-5.10 (R0rt1z2, Aug 2026): a kernel root exploit (CVE-2026-43499) giving **temporary** root on Fire Max 11 (sunstone) and Fire TV Stick 4K Max 2nd Gen (karat), both Fire OS 8; notes Amazon added KASLR on newer Fire OS builds, and the tool auto-disables OTA packages (`pm disable com.amazon.device.software.ota` etc.) because "a single OTA can move you to a build that… fixes the exploit" — [R0rt1z2/GhostLock-5.10](https://github.com/R0rt1z2/GhostLock-5.10)

### Inferences (transferability to Echo Show 8)
- Fire TV confirms the pattern the report should build on: MediaTek-based Amazon devices with a BROM flaw get *persistent* unlocks + custom recovery + full custom ROMs (LineageOS), while software-only kernel exploits (GhostLock, mtk-su) are temporary and OTA-patchable. Echo Show 8 Gen 1 (MT8163, amonet) sits firmly in the first, durable category.
- The Fire TV data also shows Amazon actively hardening *later* boot stages: the sheldon unlock required Fire OS ≤ 7.2.7.3, meaning updated preloader/LK code can defeat the *second stage* of a chain even though the BROM entry point survives. For Echo Show devices bought new-in-box in 2026 on old stock firmware the window is wide; devices on the latest OTA need the current exploit versions (amonet-crown v1.0.0 reportedly still works on latest Fire OS as of Nov 2025).
- The Aug 2026 amonet-karat release proves MediaTek BootROM-class attacks still succeed against Amazon hardware being sold new — useful against any assumption that "Amazon fixed all this years ago."
- Boot chain comparison (Fire TV vs Echo Show): same MediaTek BROM → preloader → LK → signed kernel architecture on the MediaTek models; the Fire TV-specific difference is the lack of a touchscreen/tablet assumptions and (on Sticks) harder physical access, yet exploits still shipped. The Echo Show 8 Gen 1's chain is essentially identical to karnak-class Fire tablets, which is why its unlock arrived only weeks after the Echo Show 5/Spot unlocks in Oct 2025.

### Gaps
- The exact SoC of Fire TV Stick 3/Lite (sheldon) was not confirmed in sources consulted (its exploit is BROM-based regardless).
- No evidence of any Fire TV *box* (as opposed to Stick) receiving a current amonet-class unlock; not confirmed either way.
- Whether Amazon has shipped eFuse-based anti-rollback on any Fire TV SoC — no source found (see next section).

## Echo Show 15: dev mode, sideloading, and custom OS work

### Takeaway
The Echo Show 15 never had an official developer mode for running custom software — Amazon's developer story was widgets/APL skills and, after Dec 2022, Fire TV apps. The only real "opening" was a sideloading loophole created accidentally by the Dec 2022 Fire TV update, closed by Amazon around Feb 1, 2023; since then the only route is hardware-level eMMC access, which is exactly the technique the Echo Show 8 3rd Gen community used in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- No "dev mode" was marketed: Amazon's own developer materials for Echo Show 15 (Sept 2021 launch) cover widgets, APL 1.8, and viewport profiles for skills — no OS-level developer access — [Amazon Developer Blog (Sept 2021)](https://developer.amazon.com/en-US/blogs/alexa/alexa-skills-kit/2021/09/build-with-amazons-newest-devices-and-services); third-party developer interaction with Show 15 today is "as a Fire TV app," fighting silent permission denials (RECORD_AUDIO) in Fire OS 7 — [Amazon Developer Community (Feb 2026)](https://community.amazondeveloper.com/t/echo-show-15-microphone-and-camera-access/27746)
- Amazon shipped the full Fire TV experience to Echo Show 15 as a free update Dec 7, 2022 (incl. Alexa Voice Remote support) — this turned the Show 15 into a Fire TV-class Android device and is what enabled sideloading — [About Amazon (Dec 7, 2022)](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/echo-show-15-amazon-fire-tv-software-update); [PCMag (Dec 8, 2022)](https://www.pcmag.com/news/amazon-adding-fire-tv-to-echo-show-15)
- The sideloading method (Dec 2022, popularized by the Supercar Street Racing YouTube channel): use Fire TV's hidden Developer Tools Menu + File Explorer + Downloader + Taotlus to install arbitrary Android APKs (e.g., the Home Assistant app); "As of Feb. 1st 2023, this may no longer work on new Echo Show 15s" — [Reddit r/homeassistant (Dec 2022)](https://www.reddit.com/r/homeassistant/comments/zje1at/home_assistant_android_app_can_now_be_sideloaded)
- By 2024 the loophole was fully closed and not reopened: "There was a loophole… because Amazon left the default Android 'Files' app in place with the ability to install apps from unknown sources, but that loophole has since been closed and a new one has not been found"; on the Show 15 2nd Gen the developer options "don't include an option for 'adb' or 'Apps from unknown sources'" and the built-in root file manager was removed — [Reddit r/amazonecho (Mar 2024)](https://www.reddit.com/r/amazonecho/comments/1bjp3rm/echo_15_sideloading); [XDA Forums: sideloading on Echo Show 15 2nd gen](https://xdaforums.com/t/sideloading-on-echo-show-15-2nd-generation.4768908); users report a never-connected device is needed to enable ADB at all ("I think once you update it, it's over")
- Community workarounds since then avoid sideloading entirely (e.g., keeping a HA dashboard open in the Silk browser) — [Home Assistant community](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/keep-ha-dashboard-open-on-newer-echo-show-15-without-sideloading-anything/605542)
- Echo Show 15 1st Gen hardware: Amlogic octa-core Cortex multimedia SoC + Amazon AZ2, 2GB LPDDR4 + 1GB LPDDR4x, ~16GB Kioxia TLC NAND (TechInsights teardown); Wikipedia lists it as "Amlogic PopcornA + Amazon AZ2" — [TechInsights teardown via Electronics360 (Jul 2022)](https://electronics360.globalspec.com/article/18358/techinsights-teardown-amazon-echo-show-15); [Wikipedia: Amazon Echo Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Echo_Show)
- Hardware-level route: the `jcrasselt/amazon-echo-show-15` GitHub repo documents eMMC access on the Show 15 (partition map incl. `mmcblk0p15`/`p16` Linux partitions, backing up partitions, and an ADB-key injection trick into `/data/misc/adb` that yields a `shell` uid=2000 user with SELinux enforcing, sideload possible but no root and no /system writes due to dm-verity EIO) — referenced as the method source in the Echo Show 8 3rd Gen root thread — [XDA: [ROOT][SYSTEM][HARDWARE] Amazon Echo Show 8 3rd Gen (Rhodes/Pop1) via UART and eMMC (Jun 29, 2026)](https://xdaforums.com/t/root-system-hardware-amazon-echo-show-8-3rd-gen-rhodes-pop1-via-uart-and-emmc.4793346/)
- That same June 2026 thread states the technique extends to "a newer Echo 15 with the same processor" (i.e., the Show 15 2nd Gen sharing the Show 8 3rd Gen's Amlogic SoC), though untested by the author — [XDA (Jun 29, 2026)](https://xdaforums.com/t/root-system-hardware-amazon-echo-show-8-3rd-gen-rhodes-pop1-via-uart-and-emmc.4793346/)
- Historical note: the original Echo Show (2017) ran Android/Fire OS with hidden developer options (tap serial number), but they were "very sparse" — no ADB, no unknown-sources toggle — showing Amazon deliberately stripped Android's usual debug surface from Echo hardware from the start — [AFTVnews (Jun 28, 2017)](https://www.aftvnews.com/amazons-echo-show-runs-android-and-has-developer-options)

### Inferences (transferability to Echo Show 8)
- The Show 15 story is Amazon's most instructive security *policy* case: when a loophole appears, Amazon closes it within weeks (Feb 2023) and keeps it closed. Any soft mod on an Echo Show (incl. Show 8) must assume a short lifespan; only boot-ROM-level or hardware-level methods persist.
- The Show 15 sideloading window existed *only because* Amazon bolted the standard Fire TV stack (with its hidden Developer Tools Menu) onto an Echo device. The Echo Show 8 never got that stack on Gen 1, so it never got the equivalent loophole; conversely its MT8163 boot chain made the hard route easy. The two devices fail open in opposite directions.
- The eMMC/UART technique documented on Show 15 and perfected on Echo Show 8 3rd Gen (Rhodes, Amlogic T7/A311D2, Fire OS 7.5.7.2/Android 9, kernel 5.4.86: persistent root surviving reboots, SELinux permissive, CapBnd full, "run Debian or Alpine Linux via pivot_root") is the strongest current evidence that *modern* Echo Show hardware can run full custom Linux — but at the cost of opening the case, pogo-pad eMMC access, and partition surgery, with no BROM-style shortcut — [XDA (Jun 29, 2026)](https://xdaforums.com/t/root-system-hardware-amazon-echo-show-8-3rd-gen-rhodes-pop1-via-uart-and-emmc.4793346/)
- For the report: the Echo Show 8 Gen 3 hardware root means "custom Linux on an Echo Show 8" is *already demonstrated*, just not on the MT8163 Gen 1 — where instead the demonstrated path is amonet + LineageOS 18.1.

### Gaps
- No primary confirmation of Wikipedia's "Amlogic PopcornA" name for the Show 15 1st Gen SoC beyond Wikipedia itself; TechInsights confirms only "Amlogic octa-core."
- Conflicting SoC attributions for the Echo Show 15 2nd Gen: Wikipedia lists "AZ3 Pro with AI Accelerator" (which describes the AI engine, not necessarily the application SoC), while the XDA Show 8 3rd Gen thread implies it shares the Show 8 3rd Gen's Amlogic application processor — unresolved.
- AFTVnews comment (Oct 2025) on the Echo unlock wave: "So still nothing for the Show 15 first gen?" — no public bootloader unlock for Show 15 1st Gen found as of this research; only the eMMC/ADB-key hardware route.

## Were the MT8163 exploits (mtk-su, amonet) patched via eFuse / bootloader updates, and what does that imply for updated Echo Show 8s?

### Takeaway
No evidence exists that Amazon (or MediaTek) ever used burnt eFuses against these exploits on Amazon hardware. The documented countermeasures are: (1) OTA patches to the *software* stages (kernel, Android — which patched mtk-su-class bugs), (2) newer BootROM revisions in *newer* SoC generations (silicon fix at design time, not per-device fuses), and (3) simply shipping later devices on different SoCs. Because the MT8163 BROM flaw is unpatchable on shipped silicon, updated Echo Show 8 Gen 1 devices remain unlockable — as directly confirmed by users unlocking on the latest Fire OS in Nov 2025.

### Cited Findings
- CVE-2020-0069 (the mtk-su-class Command Queue driver flaw) was fixed by a **software** patch in the March 2020 Android Security Bulletin — it affected the running kernel, not the BootROM, so an OTA fixed it; it was still listed in CISA KEV on Nov 3, 2021 due to in-the-wild use — [Android Security Bulletin March 2020 via CVE tracker](https://notcve.org/cve/CVE-2020-0069)
- amonet by contrast is "a MediaTek **bootrom** exploit and a LittleKernel bootloader exploit" — the BootROM stage is mask ROM, which cannot be rewritten by any update; the postmarketOS wiki describes amonet as an LK exploit "installed by a bootrom exploit," i.e., the BROM flaw is the permanent foothold — [xyzz/amonet](https://github.com/xyzz/amonet); [postmarketOS wiki](https://wiki.postmarketos.org/wiki/Amazon_Fire_7_2019_%28amazon-mustang%29)
- Direct evidence that OTA updates do not block amonet on Echo hardware: user report of unlocking "Amazon Echo Show 8 Gen1, Installed to latest OTA FireOS, Used amonet-crown-v1.0.0" (Nov 2025); the crown ROM thread's install flow includes users who took the latest OTA before unlocking — [XDA crown ROM thread, p.3](https://xdaforums.com/t/rom-unofficial-11-crown-lineageos-18-1-for-the-amazon-echo-show-8-2019.4766709/page-3)
- MediaTek *did* fix BootROM flaws in later SoC silicon: users of the bypass utility report "Brom has been patched and none of the exploits work" on recent SoCs (e.g., MT6789), and the utility's supported list (mt8127/8163/8167/8173/…) is a fixed set of older chips — a design-time silicon fix, not a per-device fuse burn — [XDA MTK Auth Bypass thread](https://xdaforums.com/t/mod-dev-mediatek-mtk-auth-bypass-sla-daa-utility.4232377/page-10)
- Amazon's pattern of closing *software* holes fast: Fire OS 8.1.5.3 patched the Pro-me3us system-user exploit within ~1 month of release (Sept 2025 → Oct 2025); Kindle firmware 5.18.1.1.1 (June 2026) "specifically breaks the jailbreak exploit" on Paperwhite 4 / 10th-gen Kindle with "no known path back" once installed; KASLR added in newer Fire OS 8 builds to slow kernel exploits — [Liliputing (Oct 6, 2025)](https://liliputing.com/amazon-patches-the-exploit-that-supercharged-fire-tv-and-fire-tablet-hacking); [eReadersForum (Jun 2026)](https://www.ereadersforum.com/threads/amazons-5-18-1-1-1-update-closes-the-last-easy-jailbreak-door-on-two-older-kindles.13879/); [R0rt1z2/GhostLock-5.10](https://github.com/R0rt1z2/GhostLock-5.10)
- Amazon's longer-term hardware answer: move products off vulnerable silicon. Echo Show 8 Gen 2 moved to MT8183 (Wikipedia); Fire HD 10 (2019) moved to MT8183 (XDA user with bricked MT8183 unit unable to exploit); Show 15 / Show 8 Gen 3 moved to Amlogic; newest Echos move to in-house Vega OS (non-Android) — [Wikipedia: Amazon Echo Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Echo_Show); [XDA MTK Auth Bypass thread p.10](https://xdaforums.com/t/mod-dev-mediatek-mtk-auth-bypass-sla-daa-utility.4232377/page-10); [AFTVnews: Vega OS already on the new Echo Show 5](https://www.aftvnews.com/amazons-in-house-vega-os-is-already-on-the-new-echo-show-5) (linked from [AFTVnews Oct 14, 2025](https://www.aftvnews.com/amazons-older-echo-show-5-echo-spot-and-echo-dot-have-been-rooted-with-unlocked-bootloaders-and-custom-lineageos-roms/))
- Where an update *can* still matter: second-stage (preloader/LK) hardening. The Fire TV Stick 3/Lite unlock "currently requires the Firestick be on Fire OS version 7.2.7.3 or older" — updated boot-stage firmware there broke the chain's later stage even though BROM access persists; the amonet-karat thread similarly warns about firmware-version dependence and brick risk — [AFTVnews (Mar 14, 2022)](https://www.aftvnews.com/fire-tv-stick-3-and-fire-tv-stick-lite-have-been-rooted-custom-twrp-revocery-and-custom-lineageos-android-11-rom-available/); [XDA amonet-karat thread](https://xdaforums.com/t/unlock-root-twrp-unbrick-fire-tv-stick-4k-2nd-gen-series-karat-mantra.4798951)

### Inferences (transferability to Echo Show 8)
- For the Echo Show 8 **Gen 1**: the amonet BootROM foothold is permanent on every unit ever shipped; Amazon cannot revoke it with any Fire OS update. The practical risk for updated units is only that a *future* Fire OS update changes the preloader/LK in a way that forces exploit-script updates (as seen on Fire TV Sticks), not that the door closes. Users are advised by the community to block/avoid OTA only to preserve exploit-adjacent conditions and to avoid Vega-OS-style migrations.
- No eFuse/anti-rollback response was ever documented on Amazon devices — the report should state this explicitly (it's a common assumption) and attribute Amazon's actual strategy to silicon rotation + OTA software patches.
- For Gen 2 (MT8183): the MT8163 BROM flaw simply doesn't exist there (different BootROM); unless an MT8183-class flaw surfaces publicly (none found), Gen 2 units are effectively locked, matching the "10th gen+ Fire tablets not rootable" outcome for the same generation of silicon.
- For Gen 3 (Amlogic T7): the only demonstrated route is hardware eMMC surgery (June 2026), which OTA updates also cannot revoke (it's below the OS), but which is far more invasive and brick-prone.

### Gaps
- No source confirming or denying eFuse/anti-rollback fuses on any Amazon or MT8163 device was found; absence of evidence is not proof Amazon never burned fuses — but every documented Amazon countermeasure was software or new-silicon, and no fuse-related report exists in XDA/AFTVnews coverage consulted.
- Internal Fire OS version gates for amonet-crown (e.g., whether some future Fire OS build for crown would break the current v1.0.0 scripts) are unknown; the crown unlock thread (xdaforums 4766687) was not directly fetchable (403) and was characterized via the ROM thread, the amonet fork README, and the winmutt guide.

## Other smart display platforms where full custom Linux was achieved (Nest Hub, Facebook Portal)

### Takeaway
The Google Nest Hub 2nd Gen is the cleanest "full custom Linux on a consumer smart display" precedent: a security researcher broke its secure boot via a U-Boot USB flaw and ran Ubuntu — and Google patched it by OTA within weeks, showing the cat-and-mouse dynamics differ sharply from Amazon's unpatchable-BROM situation. Facebook Portal never got a public bootloader unlock; after Meta abandoned the line, the community settled for ADB-level repurposing (custom launchers), which Meta eventually tolerated by shipping an ADB toggle — a "graceful decline" model worth noting.

### Cited Findings
- **Nest Hub 2nd Gen (elaine, Amlogic S905D2):** security researcher Frédéric Basse broke secure boot in June 2022 — found UART on the USB daughterboard, fuzzed U-Boot, found a buffer overflow triggerable via a non-standard USB block size, delivered it with a Raspberry Pi Pico acting as a malicious USB flash drive, gained pre-kernel code execution, built a custom U-Boot with secure boot disabled, a custom kernel, and repacked Ubuntu's initrd — Ubuntu 22.04 booted on the device — [Hackaday (Jun 20, 2022)](https://hackaday.com/2022/06/20/breaking-google-nest-hubs-secure-boot/); [It's FOSS (Jun 17, 2022)](https://itsfoss.com/news/ubuntu-google-nest); [Android Police (Jun 17, 2022)](https://www.androidpolice.com/guy-installed-ubuntu-linux-on-google-nest-hub); artifacts: [frederic/elaine-bootimg](https://github.com/frederic/elaine-bootimg) and [frederic/elaine-linux](https://github.com/frederic/elaine-linux)
- Google's response was an OTA patch rolled out promptly after disclosure; the underlying U-Boot bug had actually been fixed upstream as early as 2019, but Google had shipped an old version — and by the time of Hackaday's writeup the window was already closing ("The bug seems to have been patched for half a year now") — [Liliputing (Jun 16, 2022, updated)](https://liliputing.com/hacker-installs-ubuntu-on-a-google-nest-hub-2nd-gen-smart-display); [Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/guy-installs-ubuntu-linux-on-google-nest-hub)
- Nest Hub 1st Gen: moved to Fuchsia in 2021; its Amlogic "Worldcup" USB service is password-protected (`password.bin` required), and while fastboot *allows* flashing a recovery image, booting it fails with "Hash of data does not match digest in descriptor" (verified/AVB-checked) — no public unlock — [LibreELEC forum (Sept 2021)](https://forum.libreelec.tv/thread-24581-hacking-the-google-nest-hub)
- Nest Hub Max (Amlogic T931, Fuchsia): only partial community progress (UART access achieved via USB/power-adapter board pins); no public root — [XDA Forums thread](https://xdaforums.com/t/google-nest-hub-max-fuchsia-amlogic-t931-meson-sherlock-g12b_newman.4622707)
- **Facebook Portal (Qualcomm QCS605, Android 9, codename aloha; discontinued by Meta):** no public bootloader unlock for consumer units — an XDA member with a *developer* unit reports consumer devices are "sealed" before shipment and the ADB entitlement "is managed through facebook's servers and only allowed to certain employees" — [XDA: Anyone been able to do anything with a Facebook Portal? p.6](https://xdaforums.com/t/anyone-been-able-to-do-anything-with-a-facebook-portal.3878505/page-6)
- After Meta abandoned Portal, ADB options began appearing in consumer device settings ("Many users are reporting that the ADB option suddenly appeared in their device settings"), enabling custom launchers (Immortal) and the Home Assistant companion app — [How-To Geek (Jun 16, 2026)](https://www.howtogeek.com/failed-facebook-smart-display-perfect-home-assistant-dashboard)
- The `unmetaportal` community tool (tested on Portal+ firmware 1.44.4, Oct 2025) converts Portals into plain Android devices purely via ADB package/app-state commands + the CVE-2024-31317 path for account cleanup — explicitly "not a firmware flash and it does not unlock the bootloader" — [fcjr/unmetaportal](https://github.com/fcjr/unmetaportal)
- A Home Assistant forum claim that "the bootloader has been cracked" on Portal is from an anonymous user with no linked exploit and should be treated as unverified — [HA community (Jan 2026)](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/can-facebook-portal-be-repurposed-as-home-assistant/977511)
- **In the Amazon corner, the closest "fully unlocked" devices today are the MT8163 Echo/Fire family itself:** R0rt1z2's unlock wave covers Echo Show 5 (1st/2nd gen), Echo Show 8 (1st gen), Echo Spot, Echo Dot 2nd gen (AFTVnews, Oct 14, 2025), with LineageOS 18.1 ROMs for checkers/rook/crown and /e/OS + camera patches following through early 2026; plus the Aug 2026 amonet-karat for current-gen Fire TV Sticks — [AFTVnews (Oct 14, 2025)](https://www.aftvnews.com/amazons-older-echo-show-5-echo-spot-and-echo-dot-have-been-rooted-with-unlocked-bootloaders-and-custom-lineageos-roms/); [amazon-oss/releases](https://github.com/amazon-oss/releases/releases)

### Inferences (transferability to Echo Show 8)
- Nest Hub vs Echo Show 8 is the sharpest contrast for the report: Google's boot chain (Amlogic SoC, U-Boot, AVB-style checks) failed *open* at the bootloader stage but was *patchable by OTA*, so the unlock window closed in weeks. Amazon's MT8163 chain failed open at the mask-ROM stage, which is unpatchable, so the Echo Show 8 Gen 1 unlock is effectively permanent. If the report wants to argue durability of a custom-Linux path on Show 8 Gen 1, the Nest Hub precedent supports the opposite conclusion for any bootloader-stage (non-BROM) flaw.
- The Nest Hub work also validates the "custom U-Boot + custom kernel + repacked distro initrd" recipe for smart displays — directly reusable as a model for what a non-Android Linux on Echo Show 8 Gen 1 would need (firmware for touchscreen etc.), complementing the LineageOS route that already exists.
- Portal shows the failure mode when no boot-chain flaw exists: community settles for launcher-level repurposing and waits for the vendor to die; the only "custom OS" outcome is a stripped stock Android. This is what Echo Show 8 Gen 2/3 would look like without the eMMC hardware route.
- Nest Hub 1st gen's Fuchsia pivot parallels Amazon's Vega OS pivot for new Echo hardware (AFTVnews): both vendors are leaving Android/AOSP on smart displays, which will end the LineageOS-style pathway on future Echo devices — a key forward-looking point for the report.
- Overall ranking of "fully unlocked" smart displays as of Oct 2026: (1) Amazon MT8163 Echo family (persistent BROM unlock + custom recovery + shipping ROMs — the strongest, ironically), (2) Nest Hub 2nd gen (full custom Linux demonstrated but OTA-patched), (3) Fire TV Sticks (persistent unlock, ROMs), (4) Portal (launcher-level only), (5) Nest Hub 1st gen/Max (nothing public).

### Gaps
- No comparable hack is known for Lenovo Smart Display / other Google-Assistant smart displays — not researched in depth here (outside suggested sources; nothing surfaced incidentally).
- Current (2026) status of the Nest Hub 2nd gen U-Boot patch coverage — i.e., whether any firmware versions still shipped vulnerable — unknown; sources only establish Google rolled out a patch in 2022.
- Whether Meta's ADB rollout to Portal devices is complete worldwide or still gradual — sources (How-To Geek, Jun 2026) describe it as ongoing.
