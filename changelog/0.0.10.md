## Major
### Isocast on Linux
The first Linux release: a `.deb` for Ubuntu 24.04 and Debian 12 (x86-64 and arm64) and an Ubuntu 24.04 tarball. First launch offers to download Cisco's OpenH264 as your user. Screens and single windows are chosen in the desktop's sharing dialog or with `isocast screen pick`, each window its own ISO (window sources thanks to Mateusz Tymek). Previews come through shared memory, the virtual camera feeds a v4l2loopback device, and web pages render in a WebKitGTK helper (Ubuntu 24.04 only). Updates are manual.

## Improvements
- On Linux the title bar is dark, the menu bar uses the app's own menus, shortcuts use Linux key names (Ctrl, Alt, Super), and Isocast has its own icon at every size.
- On Linux with an NVIDIA GPU, 1080p60 records the program and an ISO without dropped frames (tested on a Tesla T4).
- On Linux, Properties and Filters show the live source, and Open and Export use the desktop's file chooser.
- On Linux, the first run and permissions ask for nothing up front, and the camera row copies the command that gives your user access.
- Copy buttons say whether they worked.
- On Linux, NV12-only cameras are listed and record, an infrared sensor no longer appears as a second camera, a USB webcam recorded with its own microphone starts cleanly, and camera access is reported as allowed or denied. Thanks to Mateusz Tymek.
- `isocast doctor` on Linux checks real-time scheduling for the PipeWire server and for Isocast's audio capture.
- While you record, stream or keep a replay buffer and the program keeps missing frames, previews step down to 15 fps, 5 fps, then pause, and come back once it keeps up.
- Flip Horizontal and Flip Vertical in a source's Transform menu; ISO files keep the picture as captured. Thanks to Mateusz Tymek.
- Web page sound is captured on the page's own audio thread, and any gap left is counted as an audio dropout.
- Help › Report a Bug… sends a report even when the engine can't start.
## Fixes
- Web page sound keeps its silence when a page pauses or switches its audio.
- Web pages can't end a recording by sending malformed sound.
- The Removed toast with Undo appears only once the change is applied. Thanks to Mateusz Tymek.
